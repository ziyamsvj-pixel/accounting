# Expense Allocation

Support shared costs across:
- taxable income
- exempt income
- zero-rate income
- lump-sum-tax activities
- rental activity
- common/shared activity

Allocation bases:
- revenue ratio
- area ratio
- headcount
- actual consumption
- cost center
- project
- documented custom basis

Store gross cost, basis, percentages, amounts, responsible user, evidence and
applicable rule/version. Do not activate a tax treatment until its official source
has been verified.
